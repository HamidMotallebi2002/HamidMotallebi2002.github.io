About Me

I am a second-year M.Sc. student in Mechanical Engineering – Energy Conversion at the University of Tehran (current GPA: 17.88/20). I earned my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at the same university (16.81/20). My research combines CFD, numerical modeling, and thermodynamic analysis to investigate practical fluid-flow and energy-conversion problems. My anticipated M.Sc. graduation is July 2027.

Research Experience

Two-phase ejector-expansion refrigeration systems; ventilation and radiant cooling; computational fluid mechanics and hydrodynamic systems.

Methods & Software

MATLAB, Python, C++; ANSYS Fluent and CFX, COMSOL, EES; finite volume and finite difference methods, multigrid and iterative solvers.
