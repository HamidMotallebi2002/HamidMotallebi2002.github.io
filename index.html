Master’s Research

M.Sc. Thesis · Research in Progress

Numerical Modeling and Parametric Analysis of a Combined Impinging Jet Ventilation–Radiant Cooling System under High Latent Loads

The ongoing M.Sc. thesis is directed at developing and validating a coupled computational model of three-dimensional room airflow, convective and radiative heat transfer, and water-vapor transport in a hybrid impinging-jet ventilation and radiant-cooling system. Under elevated latent loads, the work will investigate moisture stratification, dew-point and condensation constraints, thermal comfort, and energy performance. A subsequent parametric analysis is planned to identify suitable operating ranges. These are proposed research objectives, not completed numerical findings.

Research focus

  • Impinging-jet ventilation and room airflow distribution
  • Radiant cooling and indoor thermal conditions
  • Thermal comfort and energy-performance assessment
  • Computational modeling and parametric analysis
