Academic Profile

I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (Energy Conversion) at the University of Tehran, where I previously received my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering. My work brings together numerical fluid mechanics, thermal-system modeling and engineering performance analysis.

My current master's research investigates a combined impinging-jet ventilation and radiant-cooling system under high latent loads. My undergraduate research examined the thermodynamic performance and preliminary design of a two-phase ejector-expansion refrigeration cycle.

I am interested in funded doctoral research opportunities beginning in Fall 2027.

Numerical Fluid Mechanics
