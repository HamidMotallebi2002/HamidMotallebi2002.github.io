Project Overview

A numerical engineering study of a Francis turbine designed for conditions associated with the Siah Bisheh hydropower plant. The project combines geometric design, mesh generation, steady CFD and turbomachinery performance calculations.

Engineering tools and methods: CFturbo, ANSYS TurboGrid, ANSYS CFX, CFD-Post.

02

Methodology

1.

Modeling and formulation

Developed a high-head runner geometry and blade definitions in CFturbo.

2.

Numerical implementation

Generated a structured, single-periodic-passage grid using TurboGrid.

3.

Analysis and comparison

Applied turbulent-flow calculations in ANSYS CFX with an SST k–ω turbulence model.

4.

Interpretation and reporting

Evaluated the numerical flow field, torque and runner-passage performance using CFD-Post.
